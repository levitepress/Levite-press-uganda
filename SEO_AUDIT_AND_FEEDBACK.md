# Levite Press Uganda — SEO / Website Feedback

## What I checked
- The supplied V4 source files and existing Supabase-related code.
- Web search visibility for the exact domain and service phrases.
- Current Uganda competitors appearing for website-design and related service searches.

## Main findings
1. The official domain is very new and did not surface prominently for exact-domain/service searches. This is normal for a new site, but indexing must be actively verified.
2. Strong Uganda competitors use dedicated pages for individual services instead of putting every service into one paragraph/card section. Examples include Kico Web Design, Web Systems Uganda, Marv Web Design and other established providers.
3. Competitor pages repeatedly make clear what they do, where they serve, how to start, and what evidence they have. Levite Press should follow the useful structure without copying unsupported claims or statistics.
4. Levite Press should compete first for realistic service-intent searches such as "website design Uganda", "website designer Kampala", "online secretarial services Uganda", "app creation Uganda", "software development Uganda", "online computer training Uganda" and related long-tail searches. Ranking is not guaranteed.
5. Because Levite Press is online-first and home-based, the website should describe Kasangati/Masooli as the operating base and Kampala/Wakiso/Mukono/Jinja as priority service areas, not imply that it has branches or a walk-in office there.
6. The resource centre should remain small until genuine useful articles are ready. Publishing large amounts of filler content can make the site less trustworthy.

## Search / indexing actions for the owner
1. Verify the domain in Google Search Console.
2. Submit `https://levitepress.online/sitemap.xml`.
3. Request indexing for the homepage and each important service page after deployment.
4. Keep the site reachable over HTTPS and make sure the canonical domain is consistent.
5. Create/maintain accurate business information on legitimate local and industry profiles. Do not claim a public walk-in office if one does not exist.
6. Add genuine portfolio projects as they become available. Case studies are much stronger than invented testimonials.
7. Publish useful resources gradually and link them to relevant service pages.
8. Monitor actual search queries and improve pages from real Search Console data instead of guessing at keywords.

## Trust warning observed during the review
A third-party website checker currently gives the very new domain a poor trust score and reports suspicious/phishing signals. This may be a false positive or a consequence of the domain being extremely new, but it should be investigated before aggressively promoting the domain. Verify DNS, GitHub Pages/domain settings, HTTPS, ownership and Search Console, and make sure no unexpected redirects or injected files exist.
