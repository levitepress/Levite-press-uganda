# V7 Amendment Notes

V7 is the next working foundation after V6. The most visible change is the removal of the old hero mockup from the live design. The homepage now presents Levite Press as an online-first digital company rather than as a walk-in office or a technology school.

The service list is deliberately not expanded beyond the current V6 list until the owner supplies the final services. Document typing remains a sub-service of Online Secretarial Services.

Levite Tools is represented as a future product area only. The long-term architecture is intended to support self-service document tools and, later, a customer workspace for orders, files and support.
