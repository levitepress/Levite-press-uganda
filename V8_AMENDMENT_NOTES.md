# V8 Amendment Notes — Service Consolidation

V8 consolidates the public service offer into five non-overlapping core areas:

1. Website Design & Development
2. Software & App Development
3. Online Secretarial Services (Document Typing remains a sub-service)
4. Online Computer Training
5. Database & Digital Solutions

Removed as public standalone categories:
- Online Services (too vague)
- Other Digital Solutions (too broad)
- Software Developer (job title rather than service)
- App Creation as a separate category (now grouped with software)
- Database Management as a separate category (now grouped with digital solutions)

Absolute/unverified promotional claims such as 100% satisfaction, 500+ students, 50+ courses and 200+ projects are not used.

Legacy service URLs are retained as noindex redirect pages so old links do not become dead ends, while the sitemap contains only canonical public pages.

The online-first model remains central: based in Kasangati, Masooli, Wakiso; priority markets Kampala, Wakiso, Mukono and Jinja; wider service across Uganda, East Africa and internationally where the service can be delivered remotely.

Levite Tools remains future/coming-soon and is not presented as an active service.
