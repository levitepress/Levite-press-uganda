ABOUT LEVITE PRESS

Online-first service, grounded in Uganda.

Levite Press Uganda provides practical digital, secretarial, technology and computer training services through online communication and delivery.

How we operate

Levite Press is based in Masooli, Kasangati area, Wakiso District, but the business is designed to serve beyond a physical location. Clients can contact us, submit requirements and receive agreed work or training online.

For work that genuinely requires physical attention, on-site support may be considered where practical and agreed in advance. The normal operating model remains remote.

Our service reach

Local priority: Kasangati/Masooli and the Kampala–Wakiso–Mukono metropolitan area.

Wider: clients across Uganda and East Africa.

International: online clients outside the region where the requested service can be delivered remotely.

OUR APPROACH

Clear service, practical results.

We focus on clear communication, practical solutions and agreed service scope. Customers receive straightforward guidance on requirements, pricing, production and delivery before work begins.
