ABOUT LEVITE PRESS

Online-first service, grounded in Uganda.

Levite Press Uganda provides practical digital, secretarial, technology and computer training services through online communication and delivery.

How we operate

Levite Press is based in Kasangati, Masooli, Wakiso District, but the business is designed to serve beyond a physical location. Clients can contact us, submit requirements and receive agreed work or training online.

For work that genuinely requires physical attention, on-site support may be considered where practical and agreed in advance. The normal operating model remains remote.

Our service reach

First priority: Kampala, Wakiso, Mukono and Jinja.

Wider: clients across Uganda and East Africa.

International: online clients outside the region where the requested service can be delivered remotely.

OUR PRINCIPLE

Useful work, honest claims.
