Online Computer Training

Practical digital skills training delivered remotely, with learning arranged around the learner’s needs and level.

  • File and folder management
  • Word processing and document preparation

  • Internet and online work skills
  • Other agreed digital skills based on the learner’s requirement

Training matched to your level
