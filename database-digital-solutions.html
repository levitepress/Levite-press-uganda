WHAT WE DELIVER

Practical database management, not just data storage

A useful database starts with understanding the information, people, workflow and result required. We review the requirement and propose an appropriate structure rather than assuming every organisation needs the same system.

  • Database planning and design
  • Structured record and information organisation
  • Data entry and data-processing workflows where agreed
  • Existing record or spreadsheet organisation
  • Database-related web and digital system requirements
  • Information retrieval and reporting considerations
  • Selected digital solutions based on the requirement
  • Ongoing improvements or maintenance discussions where suitable
