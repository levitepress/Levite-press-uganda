Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

1. Who we are

Levite Press Uganda is a digital, printing, graphic design, typing, typesetting, technology and training service operating from Masooli, Wakiso District, Uganda. For privacy questions, contact levitepressug@gmail.com or +256 786 665 412.

2. Our privacy approach

We aim to handle personal data lawfully, fairly and transparently and to collect only information reasonably necessary for a stated purpose. Our primary legal framework is Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 and the Data Protection and Privacy Regulations, 2021. Where another law applies to a particular client, transaction or jurisdiction, we will take account of applicable requirements.

3. Information we may collect

  • Name and contact details such as phone/WhatsApp number and email address.
  • Service requirements, quotations, project information and communications.
  • Application information submitted through our education/application forms.
  • Files, photographs or documents that a customer chooses to send for printing, typing, design, typesetting or another requested service.
  • Technical information necessary to operate and secure the website.

4. Why we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, provide requested services, communicate about an order or application, deliver or arrange collection of work, maintain records where necessary, protect our systems, and meet legal or regulatory obligations.

5. Customer documents and sensitive information

Please send only information needed for the requested service. Do not send passwords, payment-card PINs or other unnecessary confidential credentials. If a document contains sensitive personal information, we will use it only as reasonably necessary for the agreed service and restrict access to authorised persons.

6. WhatsApp and email

When you choose to contact us through WhatsApp or email, your information is also handled by those communication providers under their own terms and privacy practices. We do not control their systems. Our website does not require you to create a social-media account to make an enquiry.

7. Service providers

We may use trusted technology providers for hosting, databases, authentication, storage, email or other technical functions. Access is limited to what is reasonably necessary for the service. We do not sell customer personal data.

8. Retention and deletion

We retain personal information and customer files only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which they were collected, ongoing service/support, accounting or legal obligations, dispute resolution, or security. Completed customer files should be deleted or securely disposed of when they are no longer reasonably required, subject to lawful retention requirements.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including access controls and database security measures. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so customers should use reasonable care when sending highly sensitive information.

10. Your rights

Subject to applicable law and lawful limitations, you may have rights to request information about processing, access your personal data, request correction of inaccurate data, object to or restrict certain processing, and request deletion where applicable. Contact us using the details above so we can assess and respond to the request.

11. Complaints and regulatory rights

If you believe your personal data has been mishandled, contact Levite Press first so we can investigate and respond. You may also have the right to complain to Uganda's Personal Data Protection Office (PDPO), the competent data-protection authority in Uganda.

12. Children and student information

Where a service or application involves a child or student, we expect the appropriate parent, guardian, school or other lawful authority to provide or authorise information where required by law. We do not intentionally request unnecessary information about children.

13. Cookies and tracking

This website is designed to operate without advertising cookies or unnecessary tracking. If analytics, advertising or other non-essential tracking is introduced later, this notice will be updated and any required choices or notices will be provided.

14. Changes

We may update this policy when our services, technology or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page with its update date.

This policy is intended to describe our current practices and is not a substitute for legal advice.
