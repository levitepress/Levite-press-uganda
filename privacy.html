PRIVACY & DATA PROTECTION

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

This policy is written for Levite Press Uganda's website and digital service workflows with reference to Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 and the Data Protection and Privacy Regulations, 2021. It is an operational website policy, not a substitute for legal advice or a formal compliance assessment.

1. Who we are

Levite Press Uganda operates from Masooli, Wakiso, Kasangati Town Council, Uganda. Privacy enquiries can be sent to levitepressug@gmail.com or WhatsApp +256 786 665 412.

2. Information we may collect

  • Name and contact details when you request a quotation or service.
  • Project information and files that you intentionally upload or send to us.
  • Technical information needed to operate and secure the website, such as browser, device and basic server logs.
  • Information you provide when enrolling for lessons or communicating about a project.

3. Why we use information

We use information only for legitimate and stated purposes such as responding to enquiries, delivering requested services, processing project files, providing lessons, publishing approved resources, improving the website, preventing abuse and meeting legal obligations.

4. Lawful processing and consent

Where consent is required, we will seek it before collecting or using personal data for that purpose. Where another lawful basis applies, processing may occur as permitted by law. We will not normally reuse personal data for an unrelated purpose without an appropriate lawful basis.

5. Files and downloads

Files uploaded for a project or to the resource centre should not contain sensitive personal information unless the service specifically requires it and appropriate safeguards have been agreed. Published resources may be accessible to anyone who can reach the public download link. Do not upload confidential material to a public resource area.

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers that help us host, store, secure or deliver a requested service, or where disclosure is required or permitted by law. Where processors handle personal data for Levite Press, appropriate contractual and security measures should be used.

7. Security and retention

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the service, including access controls and restricted administrative access. Personal data and project files should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected, contractual needs, dispute handling, security, accounting or legal obligations.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law and any lawful limitations, you may ask about personal data held about you, request correction of inaccurate information, ask about deletion or retention, and raise a concern about how information is handled. Requests can be sent to levitepressug@gmail.com.

9. Children

Where services involve children or their personal data, appropriate parental or guardian consent and safeguards should be used as required by Ugandan law.

10. Cookies and analytics

This starter website does not require advertising cookies. If analytics, marketing pixels, forms, payment tools or other tracking technologies are added later, this policy should be updated and any required consent mechanisms implemented.

11. Data protection registration

Where Levite Press's actual processing activities fall within registration requirements, Levite Press should maintain the appropriate registration and compliance arrangements with Uganda's Personal Data Protection Office.

12. Changes

This policy may be updated when services, technology or legal requirements change. The date at the top will be updated when material changes are made.
