Privacy Policy

Our privacy approach is designed to minimise unnecessary collection, storage and sharing of customer information.

1. Privacy-first Levite Tools

Where an automatic Levite Tool supports local browser processing, the selected file is processed on the user's device. Levite Press does not need to receive, upload or store that file on its servers. The finished result is presented to the user for viewing, saving or sharing.

This applies to supported functions such as image compression, image-to-PDF conversion, PDF merging, PDF splitting and browser OCR. Availability can depend on the browser, device and file format.

2. Saving and sharing

Users are responsible for saving finished files to their own device or preferred storage service. Closing or refreshing a browser page may remove temporary working data, so important results should be saved promptly.

3. Professional services

Typing, design, complex conversion, manual OCR and other professional services may require a customer to send a file or other information to Levite Press. We only request information reasonably needed to understand, perform, communicate about and deliver the requested service.

When you choose to submit a professional request, the information you deliberately send may include your name, email address, telephone number, instructions, payment status and files required for the job.

3A. Levite Press customer account and Google sign-in

For professional online services, Levite Press plans to offer one customer workspace using Sign in with Google. Google authenticates the Google Account and provides an ID token that our server can verify. The Google sub value is intended to be the stable unique identifier for that Google Account; it is not the same thing as a government identity number and it does not give Levite Press a general right to obtain additional identity information from Google. We will not use the email address as the permanent account identifier.

Our intended customer record is deliberately small: a Levite Press customer ID, the verified Google account identifier, account status and service references. Additional information such as a phone number or service-specific details is requested only when it is reasonably necessary for the requested service.

Levite Press will not request Google Drive, Gmail, Contacts, Photos or other Google API access merely to provide sign-in. Any future permission that is not necessary for authentication will require a separate explanation and appropriate consent.

3B. What Google verification means

A successful Google sign-in confirms that Google has authenticated the account presented to the service. It does not mean that Levite Press has independently verified the person's government identity. Where a higher level of verification is genuinely necessary for a service, we may request an additional verification step and explain why it is required.

4. No unnecessary cloud storage

We do not intend to use cloud storage as a default destination for documents processed by automatic Levite Tools. Any future online storage or customer workspace feature will be introduced with clear notice, appropriate security controls and an updated privacy explanation.

5. Retention

Professional-service records and files should only be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the stated business purpose, legal obligations, dispute handling or agreed customer support. Where a retention period is established for a particular service, it should be communicated to the customer.

6. Security

We aim to use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. Customers should avoid sending highly sensitive information unless it is necessary for the requested service.

7. Information from website visits

Visiting the website does not by itself mean that Levite Press receives the contents of files selected inside a local browser tool. Basic technical information may be handled by the website hosting, security, browser or service providers as necessary to operate the website. We aim to minimise information that is not needed for our services.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as Levite Press adds services or changes how information is handled. The current version will be published on this page.
