Serving clients across Uganda and East Africa

Our operating base is Masooli, Wakiso District, Uganda. We serve customers across Uganda and consider East Africa as our first regional expansion area. Digital work can be delivered online; printing and physical orders can be collected or delivered by arrangement.

Uganda-wide & East Africa delivery

Our digital services can reach customers anywhere in Uganda through online communication. For printing, graphic design and typesetting, customers can send files by WhatsApp and arrange collection or delivery. East Africa orders are considered based on production and courier logistics.

Online service does not mean every request can automatically be accepted. We review the requirement first and confirm whether it is within our current services.

LOCAL PRESENCE

Based in Masooli, serving the metropolitan region

Levite Press is based in Masooli in the Kasangati area of Wakiso District. We do not claim branches in Kampala, Mukono or other towns. Instead, our location allows us to reach clients across the Kampala–Wakiso–Mukono metropolitan region conveniently.

For suitable local orders or client meetings, we may meet clients at an agreed location with low-cost or, where practical, no additional transport charge. This is arranged case by case and depends on the order, location and schedule.

Online orders, wider reach

Printing, graphic design, typing and typesetting files can be sent to us through WhatsApp or email. Digital services can also be requested and delivered online.
